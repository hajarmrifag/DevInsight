# Proposed fieldwork checklist

Status: planning aid only. No interviews have taken place.

## Before recruitment

- Confirm research owner, purpose, participant population, consent language, privacy review, retention policy, incentive, and accessibility plan.
- Keep participant contact details in an approved restricted system, never in this repository.
- Pilot the guide and define the participant-code convention.
- Separate public issue analysis findings from interview hypotheses.

## Before each session

- Confirm eligibility, time zone, accessibility needs, and voluntary participation.
- Prepare approved consent and recording language.
- Test the session link and note-taking setup.
- Remind the participant not to share confidential code, credentials, or personal data.

## During each session

- Obtain consent before research or recording begins.
- Ask about a concrete recent experience before testing hypotheses.
- Distinguish observed behavior, participant interpretation, and researcher inference.
- Do not collect unnecessary personal information.

## After each session

- Record only the participant code in analysis files.
- Store consent, recordings, and contact information in approved restricted locations.
- Apply retention/deletion rules and log incentive status separately.
- Debrief emerging evidence without treating one interview as a general finding.
