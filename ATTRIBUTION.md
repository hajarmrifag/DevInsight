# Attribution and public-data handling

DevInsight analyzes brief excerpts from public GitHub issues and discussions in [`vitejs/vite`](https://github.com/vitejs/vite). Every excerpt is linked to its original source and limited to the minimum text needed for research review.

Issue and discussion content remains attributable to its original GitHub authors under the terms applicable to GitHub-hosted content. The project does not claim ownership of source posts, affiliation with Vite, endorsement by source authors, or association with MLH.

The dataset intentionally excludes usernames, email addresses, contact details, inferred demographics, and private information. Public authors are treated as authors of public reports, not consented research participants. Source URLs, identifiers, dates, short excerpts, and research notes are retained for auditability.

Project code and original project documentation are released under the repository's MIT License. External linked content is not relicensed by this repository.
